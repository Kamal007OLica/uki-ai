03

Scoring.

Each category starts at 100. A high issue costs 10, a medium 4, a low 1. The overall number weights colour and layout a little more, because that's what reviews catch first.

Grid base, minimum text size and tap target are yours to set in Settings.

04

Fixes.

Most issues have a button next to them. Press it and Uki edits the layer, then audits again so the score moves while you watch.

Every fix is a normal Figma edit. Cmd-Z undoes it.

fixesone click each
  1. 1snap sizes, positions, padding and gaps to the grid
  2. 2enlarge a tap target to the minimum, kept centred
  3. 3shift text colour toward black or white until AA passesthe hue stays, only the lightness moves
  4. ?rename default layers from what's insideFrame 42 becomes Button / Continue
  5. !turn a hand-stacked frame into auto layoutgap and padding inferred from the children

05

Optional extras.

uki ai reviewyour own key

Sends the scores, issues, fonts and colours to Claude and returns what to fix first. The layers never leave Figma.

annotateon the canvas

Drops a report card next to the frame with the six scores and the top issues, for the review thread.

reportmarkdown

Copies the whole audit as Markdown: a table of scores and every issue with its layer. Paste it in the ticket.
